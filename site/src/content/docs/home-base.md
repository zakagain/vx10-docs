#### VX-10. Where is it?


That is a good question. VX-10 does not have any single “home”. And for good reason. You see, Vectura is not a government or a country. And these aircraft are designed for governments and countries. So, whilst we do provide these aircraft to the government, we can’t put them all in one place.
