## What do I want this to feel like?


That’s a difficult question. In these early conceptual stages, that’s what I’m trying to establish. At the moment, I’ve got this.


* Chasing the sun at mach 10


* Long, flowing bodylines


* Integrated “belly” that houses the scramjets


* Long afterburner flames


* Low to the ground when gear is deployed


* Finished in a stunning **Matte Charcoal** or **Dark Metallic**
