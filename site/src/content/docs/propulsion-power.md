## Custom systems


#### Active Additive Injection System


To maintain structural integrity of the internal fuel lines, VX-10 utilizes a specialized **Nanocarbon Coating** throughout the airframe. At **Hypersonic Velocity** where fuel is used as a coolant it has to undergo a process called Thermal Degradation that creates deposits of carbon that can block the injectors. To combat this we use an ultra-smooth **Diamond-Like Carbon** as chemical film. This coating is hydrophobic, meaning we can move fuel with near-zero friction, preventing any particulates from clinging onto the inner lining of the injectors. We designed a custom system; The **Active Additive Injection System.** This will monitor pressure, while simultaneously using advanced onboard AI to determine if it should give another coating. This process can be completed whilst airborne or on the ground.


#### Supersonic Combustion (Scramjets)


Contrary to standard jet engines, **Vectura Pyrostream** has no moving parts. It works by using VX-10's massive amount of forward velocity to compress air. From Mach 5 to 10, the inlet uses **morphing surfaces** to change shape. This ensures that we are getting maximum air in, with minimal losses. VX-10 also uses **Liquid Hydrogen** to pump through engine walls before combustion. By using this method, we are able to cool the engine while simultaneously preheating the fuel, making this kind of combustion even more powerful.
