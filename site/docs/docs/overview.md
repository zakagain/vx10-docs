## Mission Capabilities

- **Hypersonic Reconnaissance** — SAR mapping at centimetre resolution
- **Global Rescue Operations** — Organ/personnel transport worldwide in <1 hour
- **Rapid Deployment** — Strategic asset repositioning at Mach 10
- **High-Altitude Research** — Mesosphere/thermosphere data collection

## Key Systems

### Airframe & Aerodynamics
- Waverider design for optimal lift-to-drag at hypersonic speeds
- Morphing inlet surfaces (Mach 5–10)
- Nanocarbon thermal protection coating

### Propulsion
- **Vectura Pyrostream** multijets — no moving parts
- Liquid hydrogen regenerative cooling
- Active Additive Injection System for injector health

### Avionics & Communications
- **Vision Flight Deck** — full situational awareness
- **Vectura Speed Panel** — 240Hz OLED HDR display
- Laser comms (primary) + encrypted data pods (emergency)
- AI edge processing with Vectura Silicon CPUs

### Pilot Systems
- **VXS-01** pressure suit — micro-tube liquid cooling (14–35°C)
- **Vision Helmet** — dual 4K 240Hz OLED (Apple Vision Pro derived technology?)
- Bio-sensing seat — ECG, respiration, stress detection, auto-climate
- Emergency vision panel — 1.5s deployment

### Safety & Redundancy
- RCS thrusters for hypersonic control authority
- Multi-layer thermal protection
- Autonomous emergency procedures
