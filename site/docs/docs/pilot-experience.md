# Pilot Experience

## Behind the controls of VX-10


VX-10 is nearly entirely digital. Equipped with a beautiful Vision Flight Deck, you can see everything, everywhere, no matter the outside conditions. In an emergency, where electronic power is lost, there is a large pane of FusedSilica or Aluminosilicate glass, covered by the same carbon composite as the whole aircraft body. The mechanism for this will split down the centre, and part. In a **fatal** emergency, the carbon composite panel will violently blow off the aircraft, leaving the pilots with a full view, in less than 1.5 seconds. It can be wound completely by hand using a machined aluminium crank, or blown out with the flick of a switch.


#### UX/UI


The aircraft also is equipped with the **Vectura Speed Panel,** a beautifully designed, 240hz, OLED HDR (Apple partnership? 🤷🏼‍♂️) screen that displays speed with standout colour changes as speed increases. I’m thinking from cool blue, to (near) red-hot (like the plane’s exterior).


#### The Seat


The seat in this aircraft is something special. It uses **Bio-Textile Sensing** that utilises contact-free sensors built into the upholstery. It monitors **ECG** and breathing. If the seat detects symptoms of **stress** or anything worse, for example a **panic attack** then it alerts the crew, and can and will trigger environmental systems to calm the pilot (or crew) down. It can use a mixture of audio therapy and massage, built into the seat. To assist, the seats also use **intelligent pneumatic valves** to correct themselves avery 25 milliseconds. During turns and manoeuvres at high speeds, the seat can tense up in specific locations to support key parts of the body. The seat is also equipped with **Auto Climate**. It senses humidity and temperature at the highest resolution. If the crew start to sweat slightly from stress, not only do aforementioned systems kick in, gentle cooling will start to acclimatise the pilot or crew.


#### The Suit


For the pilot to be able to survive at **Mach 10,** we need to design a custom-made body wearable. It contains a wide network of micro-tubes, which circulate chilled fluid so the pilot and crew stay at a comfortable, yet adjustable temperature (16°C-32°C). This connects directly to the seat's inbuilt **Liquid Suit Connector Assembly (LSCA)**, which autonomously monitor body temperature and can adjust accordingly. We call this suit, **VXS-01.**


#### The Vision Helmet


This custom-designed helmet features technology derived from the **Apple Vision Pro.** Utilising dual-4K 240hz OLED displays, it mirrors all data that anyone would **ever** need. It is fully customisable, being able to mirror the **Speed Panel** in real-time. Also, it can be completely disabled and turn fully transparent.
