# Where to Fly

## The Mesosphere


This is the standard cruising altitude for VX-10. The air is thin enough that the airframe won’t melt, but has enough oxygen left for the scramjets. This is where above is pitch-black, and below lies a thin blue line of the atmosphere.


## The Thermosphere


If VX-10 needed to get somewhere ASAP, we _could_ perform a ballistic skip into the **Lower Thermosphere.** At this height, VX-10 is essentially a low-orbiting spacecraft. We’d be looking down upon auroras, and the sun never really sets.
