# Data Links

## How VX-10 communicates with ATC


#### Laser based (Primary)


At hypersonic speeds, VX-10 cannot use traditional radio-based comms. Instead, we use a **laser emitter** located in the tail, away from the plasma covered nose. Since VX-10 operates in the **Lower Mesosphere,** we can utilise the direct line of sight to the **orbital network.** This means that we can get **gigabit** or possibly even higher speeds to beam the 3D images that VX-10's **SAR** captures and send it straight to the ground operators.


#### Emergency data "launch"


In situations where no data seems to be able to get out for whatever reason (Electronic interference, or when radio silence is required) VX-10 can deploy a **heat-shielded encrypted data pod** with exact coordinates, dropped out of the **Ventral Hatch** for immediate recovery by the team.


#### AI Edge Processing


To aid ground teams, all mapping data is processed by onboard AI in real-time, before being beamed up to a satellite and then down to ground. The pilot and crew are only notified to critical alerts, high-priority targets and environmental threats, rendered beautifully on the **Speed Panel.** VX-10 should have the power of a small data-centre, 2+ CPUs (Vectura Silicon) and (maybe) multiple NVIDIA Blackwell Superchips.
