# Example Missions


#### Operation Incision


**Scenario:** 9.6 magnitude earthquake in Japan.


The fleet of 3 VX-10’s are stationed in Area 51, Nevada, United States. The earthquake has alert has just been received by government officials. The fleet is sent out on emergency response protocol. They travel in a convoy in the **Lower Mesosphere** at Mach 10, **(approx. 7,600mph).** VX-10 arrives in 42 minutes. Despite near total darkness, SAR technology creates 3D maps of the area, at centimetre level detail. It processes all raw data with onboard AI and beams it up to satellites, which ground control can assess. The crew are regulated at 21°C. After the initial assessment, they circle back to the United States, change the configuration of the aircraft to include medical supplies and go back to the site, all within 2   hours. Rescue and supplies are then dropped down to aid the survivors.


(VX-10 were not hypersonic whilst assessing the severity of the earthquake).
