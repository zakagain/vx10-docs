## Basic Information


VX-10 is a concept aircraft capable of reaching immense speeds, and crewed hypersonic flight.


It can be used for Military, or [Global Rescue Operations](Global Rescue Operations.md).


## In case of engine failure


The secondary thrusters that turn the plane at hypersonic speeds will automatically turn on and mitigate the risk of spinning helplessly out of control. (RCS thrusters)
